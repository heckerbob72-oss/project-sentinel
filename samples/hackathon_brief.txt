PROJECT BRIEF — SentinelOps Hackathon Entry

Objective: Build an agentic AI project co-ordinator that helps hackathon teams
plan and deliver on time with explainable, evidence-backed recommendations.

Business problem: Teams routinely miss deadlines because planning is ad-hoc,
risks are spotted too late, workload is unbalanced, and testing is squeezed into
the final hours.

Team of 4 members.
- 1 backend engineer (Python, FastAPI)
- 1 frontend engineer (React, TypeScript)
- 1 AI engineer (LLMs, RAG)
- 1 designer / QA

Skills available: python, fastapi, react, typescript, ai, ux, qa.

Deliverables:
- Working backend API
- Interactive dashboard
- Multi-agent planning workflow
- Demo and pitch deck

Deadline: 2026-08-10.
Demo date: 2026-08-10.
Testing window: 2 days.

Constraints: Must run locally via Docker. Budget: 0 (hackathon).
Submission rules: code repository plus a 5-minute demo video.

Success criteria: an end-to-end plan generated from an uploaded brief, with
explainable risk detection, a project health score, and a what-if simulation.

Known risks mentioned by the team: the AI engineer is also responsible for the
demo, and integration testing is scheduled for the final day.
